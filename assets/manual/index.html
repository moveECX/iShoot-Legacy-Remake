Overview

Take control of a powerful tank armed with the latest high-tech weaponry in an artillery battle to the death. Lob devastating weapons at your foes as you attempt to be the last tank standing!

Getting Started

From two to four tanks can play, and at least one of the players must be human. Use the controls on the New Game screen to select the number of human and computer tanks as well as the length of play and difficulty of computer opponents.

Click on the button corresponding to a human player to enter a new name and select tank appearance and color. These selections are purely cosmetic and do not affect gameplay.

Controls

Touch the screen to set your aim. The tank will point its gun barrel directly at your finger. Tanks can aim anywhere from horizontal to straight up, but cannot aim any lower than horizontal.

Select your weapon by tapping the left or right arrows next to the weapon name at the bottom of the screen (you remembered to buy weapons, right?).

To shoot, press and hold the fire button. A bar will appear showing your shot strength. The longer you hold the button, the further the shot will travel.
